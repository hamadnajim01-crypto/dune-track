About DuneTrack

The Story Behind DuneTrack

Born from a passion for desert adventure and cutting-edge technology, DuneTrack was created to bring data-driven performance tracking to the sandboarding community.

Why We Built This

Sandboarding in the UAE desert is an incredible experience, but until now there was no way to track your performance. Surfers have wave data, skiers have slope apps, cyclists have Strava — sandboarders had nothing. DuneTrack fills that gap by using your phone's built-in GPS, accelerometer, and gyroscope sensors to capture every detail of your ride. No expensive gear needed — just your phone and your board.

How It Works

DuneTrack is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that runs directly in your browser — no app store download required. When you start a session, it activates your phone's GPS for real-time speed and distance tracking, the accelerometer for measuring G-forces and detecting airtime, and the gyroscope for tilt angle data. If you have a Bluetooth-enabled sandboard sensor, DuneTrack connects to it wirelessly for even more precise measurements. All your ride data is saved locally on your device, so your privacy is always protected.

Built for UAE Desert

DuneTrack is purpose-built for the UAE desert environment. It pulls real-time weather data from the Open-Meteo API for locations like Al Badayer, Liwa, and Fossil Rock, giving you temperature, wind speed, humidity, and UV index before you head out. The Eco Tracker feature helps you ride responsibly by scoring your environmental impact and guiding you to designated safe zones, protecting the delicate desert ecosystem for future generations.

Safety First

Desert Safety Tips

Sandboarding is an extreme sport. Follow these guidelines to stay safe while enjoying the dunes.

💧

Stay Hydrated

Bring at least 2 liters of water per person. The desert heat can dehydrate you quickly, especially during physical activity. Drink water regularly even if you don't feel thirsty.

☀️

Sun Protection

Apply SPF 50+ sunscreen, wear UV-protective sunglasses, and consider lightweight long sleeves. The UV index in the UAE desert can exceed 11 during peak hours.

⏰

Best Riding Times

Ride during early morning (6-9 AM) or late afternoon (4-6 PM) to avoid extreme heat. The sand temperature can exceed 70°C at midday, which is dangerous for exposed skin.

🛡️

Wear Protection

Always wear a helmet and protective eyewear. Sand can cause abrasions and eye injuries at high speed. Knee pads and gloves are recommended for beginners.

📱

Tell Someone

Always inform someone of your riding location and expected return time. Keep your phone charged and bring a portable battery pack. DuneTrack's GPS works offline once loaded.

🚗

Vehicle Access

Park your vehicle on hard ground, never on soft sand. If driving into the desert, use a 4WD vehicle with proper tires. Deflate tires for better desert traction and re-inflate before returning to roads.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about DuneTrack.

Is DuneTrack really free?

Yes, completely free. No premium tiers, no ads, no subscriptions. All features are available from day one. We built DuneTrack as a passion project for the sandboarding community.

Do I need to download anything from the App Store?

No. DuneTrack is a Progressive Web App (PWA). Just open the website in your browser and tap "Add to Home Screen" to install it. It works like a native app but without the app store.

Does it work without internet in the desert?

Yes. Once loaded, DuneTrack works offline using service worker caching. GPS tracking, speed measurement, and all sensor data work without internet. Weather updates require a connection.

What devices are supported?

Any device with a modern browser. iPhone (iOS 13+), Android phones, tablets, and desktops. For best results, use Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone. GPS and sensor features require a phone.

Do I need the Bluetooth sensor?

No. The Bluetooth sandboard sensor is optional and provides extra precision for acceleration and tilt data. Your phone's built-in GPS and accelerometer provide all the core tracking features.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. All your ride data is stored locally on your device using localStorage. Nothing is sent to any server. Your account information stays on your device too. You can export or delete your data anytime from Settings.

How accurate is the GPS speed tracking?

GPS accuracy depends on your device and conditions. In open desert with clear sky, accuracy is typically within 1-3 km/h. DuneTrack uses high-accuracy GPS mode and filters out noise to give reliable readings.

Can I use DuneTrack outside the UAE?

Yes! While DuneTrack is optimized for UAE desert locations and pulls UAE weather data, the GPS tracking, speed measurement, and sensor features work anywhere in the world.
